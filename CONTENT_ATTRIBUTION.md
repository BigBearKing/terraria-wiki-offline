license any of the following:

- Wiki pages, page metadata, images, audio, video, CSS, JavaScript, or HTML
  copied from a Wiki or downloaded by the application;

## Redistribution requirements

When distributing data or files copied from a Wiki, retain the source URL,
copyright notice, required attribution, original license version, and a notice
of modifications. Do not replace a source license with this repository's
Apache-2.0 license.

Do not assume a site-wide CC license covers every asset. The Terraria Wiki
expressly directs users to each file page for graphical and audio licensing.
Game and mod intellectual property remains with its respective owner. Obtain
permission or remove a resource if its specific license does not permit the
intended redistribution.

## Maintainer release checklist

1. Record the exact source URL and retrieval date for each bundled Wiki asset.
2. Verify each image, audio file, logo, and non-original CSS/JS file; preserve
   its notice and license in this document or in a per-file manifest.
3. Verify every Wiki's current copyright page before shipping a data package.
4. State that an exported `.pkg` contains third-party content and include this
   document with it.
